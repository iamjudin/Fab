# JTBD — operational brief

Use with [Human Jobs contract](../../skills/product/references/human-jobs.md); that contract is the maintained
Wireframer policy. Source: the maintainer's JTBD methodology research.
Detailed packet: [jtbd.full.json](jtbd.full.json).

## Working decisions

- Human Job describes meaningful progress A→B that survives removal of the
  proposed product. Context, outcome, evidence, alternative and forces have
  separate meanings. A feature or rule need not acquire a fabricated job.
- Derive contextual Job Stories from the chosen parent Human Job. Sentence
  templates do not classify an entity. Wireframer uses a stricter independence
  test for JS than some historical Job Story design examples; this is our policy,
  not a universal JTBD definition.
- Reconstruct actual episodes where available. Candidate jobs without evidence
  remain assumptions. Personal experience supports that person's situation,
  not population demand. Choose interviews, observation, diary or source analysis
  according to the claim and decision risk, without universal sample quotas.
- Split/merge by different progress, triggers, alternatives and independent
  decision episodes. A common subject or shared screen is not enough.
- Cancellation, error or recovery becomes a separate job only if it creates
  independent meaningful progress; otherwise retain it as context, UC or rule.
