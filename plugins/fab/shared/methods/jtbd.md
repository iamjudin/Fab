# JTBD — knowledge brief

Read when distinguishing a person's desired progress, functional job, use case,
feature or rule. [Preserved source](jtbd.full.json).

## Knowledge and limits

- JTBD contains different traditions. Christensen/Moesta/Klement emphasize
  progress and switching, while ODI emphasizes functional jobs and outcomes.
  Choose a lens for the question rather than treating one definition as universal.
- Keep context, outcome, evidence, alternatives and motivation distinguishable.
  A sentence template does not establish what kind of entity it describes.
- Use actual episodes when available. Candidate jobs remain assumptions without
  applicable evidence. One person's experience does not establish population demand.
- Choose interviews, observation, diaries or source analysis for the claim and
  uncertainty. No universal sample size or compulsory discovery process follows.
- Split or merge by meaningful differences in progress, trigger, alternatives and
  decision episode. Error, cancellation or recovery may be part of an existing
  task rather than a separate job. A common subject or screen proves neither.
