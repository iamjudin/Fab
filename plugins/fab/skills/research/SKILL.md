# Research
Identify the knowledge purpose or decision the evidence can change, sources/method,
sufficient scope, stop and limits. Consult existing relevant knowledge first.
Distinguish a reusable domain foundation from a focused high-uncertainty question,
with project context setting priorities rather than restricting coverage to current
features. Use existing context without a mandatory product frame/graph.
Read [independent research and application](references/reuse.md): keep new topic
research separate from consuming-project identity and local decisions, while
retaining the conditions needed for useful transfer.
For research assignments, synthesis and human briefs read the
[reader contract](references/reader-contract.md). Plan evidence and its reader
presentation together, without reducing the evidence scope to fit the layout.
For reusable knowledge choose only the relevant [method](../../shared/methods/index.md).
If deeper needed, resolve the [bundled reader](scripts/research_packet.py) from
this skill's directory, then use `read <full.json> --index` and `--section <ID>`.
Do not assume a script under the package root or current workspace. No full-library
reading or Brain dependency.
For actual research reach the promised evidence threshold with available tools;
otherwise prepare the brief/prompt and name what remains unperformed.
Honor the agreed method as well as the evidence threshold. A blocked explicitly selected
research mode is not permission to dispatch a substitute. Offer the concrete
alternative before dependent execution. Use an already authorized fallback without
another generic approval. When means are open, choose suitable available tools.
Read the [method boundary](references/deep-research.md#selected-method-and-access-failure)
for a commissioned Deep Research route.
Apply [output evidence review](references/evidence-review.md) to assignment coverage
and actual returned results. Layout compliance does not establish evidence quality. Verify
unstable consequential facts through current primary sources.
When ingesting read [ingestion contract](references/ingestion.md); original,
brief and compatible indexed full retain qualifications and provenance.
After completing ingestion, follow [additive project tags](../../shared/library-usage.md).
Use existing source-write authorization or offer the exact tag once. Keep all tags
and filenames, with technical metadata only in a hidden EOF footer. Declining
never blocks the completed research result.
Project evidence stays in project; global promotion requires a reusable gap and
explicit applicability, not a market report becoming universal knowledge.
Research findings are not creator acceptance or proof of demand. Update affected
sources/decisions only in authorized scope. Stop at usable evidence or requested prompt.
For Deep Research prompts read [output requirement](references/deep-research.md).
