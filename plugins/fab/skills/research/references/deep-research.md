# Deep Research assignment and output

## Selected method and access failure

An explicitly commissioned Deep Research investigation includes its chosen mode,
not only its topic. If that surface is blocked, preserve saved plans and observed
execution state. Distinguish restoration of the existing run, waiting for its
complete result, a creator-operated handoff and a different research method.
Recommend a concrete viable option and explain how its evidence/output boundary
changes before executing an unagreed substitute. A disclosed change is not consent.

Continue independent authorized preparation. A pre-authorized fallback can be
executed without asking again, with its limits retained. When the person leaves
research means open, choose appropriate tools autonomously. Partial material may
remain useful but cannot fulfill the original commission by being renamed,
formatted, ingested or marked complete. Do not resend an existing plan blindly.

## Assignment and output

Apply the [reader contract](reader-contract.md) when preparing the assignment.
Choose the domain-foundation or focused-gap purpose before writing the question.
State the decision or knowledge question, required evidence, transfer boundaries,
stop condition and unknowns. Add a topic-specific presentation plan: what the
reader needs to compare, scan and understand causally. Keep the full evidence
scope, but separate detailed records from the main reading path. Apply
[evidence review](evidence-review.md): require coverage or a named gap, supporting
links and coherent numerical scenarios where relevant. Model the compared option,
not an unrelated option with more convenient available numbers.
Apply [independent research and application](reuse.md). Formulate a standalone
research question with relevant topic conditions instead of the consuming project
name. For foundation work include relevant domain mechanisms and adjacent options
beyond current features. For focused work state the consequential uncertainty
and existing-evidence gap. Request independent findings, then prepare local
application separately.
Add this topic-specific instruction to the assignment, not merely to its format
footer. Preserve source names and named studied cases when they are evidence.

Append the following output contract in the requested report language. Translate
it faithfully when needed. The former verbatim Russian tail is superseded.

> Prepare clean Markdown for Obsidian in the requested language. Use meaningful
> ##/### headings and navigation for a substantial report. Begin with the answer
> or useful orientation, evidence status and the main limitation. Use compact
> tables for comparisons and repeated short fields, lists for independent items,
> numbered steps for procedures and short paragraphs for causal explanation.
> For multi-field items put fields on separate lines, not inside a long numbered
> paragraph. Keep each detailed profile once instead of repeating it before and
> after a comparison table.
> Avoid long prose inside table cells and repeated summary layers. Preserve
> counterevidence, conditions, uncertainty and source traceability. Keep detailed
> evidence in a separate linked appendix or full packet rather than crowding the
> reader view. Do not use semicolons in newly authored reader text. Preserve code
> and exact quotations. Use descriptive Markdown title-links near claims, without
> numeric citations or raw export markers. Group sources by section at the end
> under Sources, localized to the report language. Use Mermaid only when it adds
> understanding, keep it as editable code and ensure no horizontal scrolling at
> the intended note width. TD/TB alone does not ensure this. Use a table or list
> instead when it explains the relationship more clearly. Before delivery review
> the opening and a dense section for scanning, reasoning and evidence fidelity.
> Check that each required question is answered or has an explicit evidence gap.
> Reconcile numerical formulas, assumptions and tables with the compared options.
> Link material empirical claims to actual supporting evidence and state the
> report date. State any unverified rendering or unresolved source limitation.

This tail does not replace the topic-specific evidence and presentation plan.
A requested prompt is a delivered assignment, not a completed investigation.
Do not claim that an external research tool followed the contract until its
actual output has been reviewed.
