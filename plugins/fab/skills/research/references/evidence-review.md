# Research output review

Apply when designing assignments and reviewing returned research. A well-formatted
answer can still miss the assignment or contain unsupported conclusions. Request
an explicit answer or named evidence gap for each required question. Review the
actual output before treating research as usable project evidence.

## Coverage and support

Keep a small internal mapping of required questions to report sections or unresolved
gaps. Do not turn partial coverage into a complete recommendation. A missing region,
comparison, cost category or source cannot be silently replaced with a broad claim.
State what was not checked and how it limits the answer. Do not fill unavailable
parameters with plausible-looking numbers.

Material empirical claims need supporting descriptive links at the claim or an
unambiguous nearby evidence record. A list of institution names is not source
verification. Record dates and scope, and distinguish opened evidence from reported
claims. Counts of links do not establish support. If evidence is unavailable, retain
an explicit unknown instead of claiming verification.

## Conditional numerical review

When quantitative scenarios serve the question, define units, denominators,
assumptions and formulas once and carry them consistently through the report.
Check arithmetic and reconcile formulas, tables and recommendations. Keep relevant
filters in the calculation, including eligibility and actual reach when those are
claimed. Distinguish conditional potential from achievable demand.

Match the model to the option actually compared or recommended. For pricing,
subscription retention is not a model of one-off purchases. Include required cost
categories or label them unknown and report the incomplete metric accurately.
Do not call partial receipts profit or an acquisition proxy a paying-customer CAC.
Show the important sensitivity and explain any change of assumptions between views.
These checks are conditional, not a requirement to invent economics for every topic.

## Reader review and preservation

A numbered paragraph with many inline fields is still dense. Put independent fields
on separate lines under the item's heading. Do not repeat full profiles before and
after a comparison table. Keep one canonical detail location and use nearby short
prose for the causal explanation or exception the table cannot express.

Scan authored headings, cells, lists and paragraphs for prohibited semicolons.
Preserve punctuation in exact quotations and code. Verify requested detail/appendix
navigation and any actual rendering claim. Do not infer legible Mermaid from TD/TB.

Preserve supplied original results before corrections. Formatting correction does
not authorize changing claims, repairing sources or adding missing research under
Refine. Research follow-up and new evidence must be separately identified. If an
external answer misses the contract, record partial compliance and the specific
remaining work, not a PASS from tables or an attractive opening.
