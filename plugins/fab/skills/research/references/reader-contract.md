# Research reader contract

Use for new research assignments, synthesis and operational briefs. This is an
information architecture contract, not a quota for tables or a summary length.
Preserve the requested research depth and significant qualifications. Apply
[independent research and application](reuse.md) before choosing the outline.

## Plan the assignment

Identify the reader's decision or knowledge question, evidence needed to resolve
it, important contrasts, boundaries of transfer and remaining unknowns. Map each
question to a useful presentation before drafting the prompt:

| Reader task | Default presentation |
|---|---|
| Compare alternatives or repeated short evidence fields | Compact comparison table |
| Scan independent findings, conditions or open questions | List |
| Follow a procedure or sequence | Numbered steps |
| Understand cause, disagreement or limits of transfer | Short connected paragraphs |
| Inspect extensive methods, records or supporting detail | Linked full packet or appendix |

Choose columns that answer the actual comparison. Do not request every evidence
field in one wide table. Keep detailed records separately and retain source
references, important exceptions and applicability near the reader's conclusion.
Broad research can be necessary. Distinguish required questions from optional
detail and define sufficient evidence rather than making the report shallow.

## Compose the reader view

Start with the answer or useful orientation, its evidence status and the main
limit. Add navigation for substantial reports. Use meaningful sections driven
by reader questions rather than a fixed universal outline.

- Use tables for genuine comparison. Keep cells short and columns few enough
  to scan in an ordinary note pane. Move long explanations into linked detail
  or a nearby paragraph. A paragraph pasted into a cell is still dense prose.
- Use lists for independent items and inline enumerations. Preserve whether
  items are alternatives, cumulative conditions or ordered steps.
- Keep causal arguments in short paragraphs, each developing one point.
  Preserve connecting reasoning, counterevidence and qualifications. Do not
  turn every sentence into a bullet or replace every explanation with a table.
- Do not use semicolons in newly authored reader text, including headings,
  table cells and list items. Separate independent items into a list and linked
  arguments into short sentences. Preserve code and exact source quotations.
- Keep evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and accepted decisions distinct.
  Attach the relevant source and uncertainty to the claim they qualify.
- Avoid duplicated overview and detail records. A brief may select what serves
  the question, but its exclusions must remain accessible in the full material.

## Diagrams and sources

Prefer a table or list when it explains the relationship. Use Mermaid only when
it adds understanding. Keep it editable and without horizontal scrolling at the
intended note width. TD/TB direction alone is insufficient: parallel branches,
long labels and dense edges can still make a wide unreadable graph.
Simplify presentation without dropping relevant relationships, split into named
linked views when that preserves context, or use a textual relationship list.
Preserve the complete graph separately when the reader view is selective.

Use descriptive Markdown title-links near supported claims and group sources
by section at the end. Do not invent links or erase unresolved provenance.
Keep supplied originals unchanged and detailed indexed packets lossless within
their declared scope. Reader layout changes do not authorize source rewriting.

## Review before delivery

Read the opening and one representative dense section. Can the reader locate
an answer, compare alternatives and find the important exception without
extracting them from a wall of prose? Check that lists preserve logical relations,
tables support comparison and short paragraphs still explain why. Trace key
claims, numbers, negations, conditions, uncertainty and sources to full material.
Inspect table and diagram rendering at the intended width when available.
Otherwise name layout verification as unobserved. Markdown validity, word counts,
table counts and compression ratios do not establish readability or human value.
