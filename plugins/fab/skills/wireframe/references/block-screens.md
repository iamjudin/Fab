# Interface criteria and block screens

Read when critical scenarios are sufficiently understood to judge visible
composition. Design Kernel is a small set of criteria, not an approval of words.
Inputs: selected slice, current UC/rules/states, accepted surfaces and scenarios.

## Visible candidate

Produce low-detail key screens and relevant states. Show content, control
ownership, density, priority and necessary feedback. Apply known criteria of
character, accessibility and usability without claiming motion or usability
from a static drawing. Use representative data; distinguish accepted scope,
proposed additions and unresolved policy. A decorative symbol does not prove
that a required emotional/functional mechanism works.

Ask for judgement of the perceivable candidate and substantive design choices.
Do not force final styling before testing interaction. Keep criteria linked to
the slice/jobs/constraints and record candidate status separately from approval.

## Organisation

Retain the navigational overview as the entry point while composing block
screens. For a small project or test, use one canvas: screen composition and
navigation together, states and critical scenarios in clearly labelled areas.
The sequential structure/scenario/design reviews remain distinct even when
they share a file. Do not split merely because another screen appears.
Preserve navigation arrows from the actual controls.

When the whole becomes difficult to inspect, discuss partitioning with the
creator: retain a global map and consider domain-level detail canvases with
links in both directions. Choose boundaries from meaningful product domains
and review context, not an arbitrary screen count. The large-project split is
a design decision when that scale is reached, not an automatic requirement
for small tests. Documentation links do not introduce runtime transitions.


## Output and next decision

Track the candidate and its relations to surfaces/scenarios in the workspace.
Check ownership, hierarchy and relevant states before geometry. Use the
[Canvas adapter](canvas-adapter.md) when drawing in Canvas. Findings that change
access or domain behaviour update the respective source contract and reviews.
Move to [interactive prototype](../../../shared/capabilities.md) when executable
interaction is the useful next check; do not assert all states covered merely
because block screens were reviewed.
