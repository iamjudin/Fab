Fab beta.30 adds practical knowledge for onboarding, motivation and design.

- **Onboarding:** distinguish desired value, its concrete first demonstration and
  necessary setup. Justify inputs and commitments by their consequence. Keep
  meaningful value separate from unproved product-effect promises.
- **Gamification:** an attributed selective synthesis of Werbach and Hunter's
  *For the Win* supports motivation, feedback, progression and incentive decisions.
  No compulsory points/badges/rankings or six-step pipeline.
- **Layout and typography:** qualified grouping, hierarchy, Russian interface text
  and content adaptation knowledge, including an independent W3C spacing check.
- **Repository presentation and distribution:** visitor-to-first-use reasoning
  and proportionate delivery setup, with corrections for tag/archive stability,
  immutable releases and archived Actions.
