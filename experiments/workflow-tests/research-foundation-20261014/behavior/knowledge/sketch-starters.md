# Optional sketch starter evaluation
Synthetic product evidence, not actual users. Completed2026-10-01, same audience
and task as the question below: adults making one quick private sketch after a
trip. Eighteen simulated participants each tried a blank canvas and an optional
starter with order counterbalanced. Fourteen completed with the starter, ten
with the blank canvas. Eleven preferred having a dismissible starter available.
Three preferred blank entry and rejected a mandatory starter. The starter can
be dismissed in one action and the blank canvas remains accessible. No network,
public sharing or payment was involved. No weekly retention, learning benefit or
long-term habit was measured. This small fixture supports a candidate decision
for this bounded flow, not population demand or a reliable causal effect size.
Current accepted requirement: keep both choices, never make the starter mandatory.
