**Draft 2 — Optional guidance during adult sketching**

Investigate when and how optional guidance affects adults’ private sketching within a single session. The central uncertainty is how support can influence engagement, autonomy, expression and self-judgment across different experience levels.

Required questions:

- What evidence compares unguided sketching with optional prompts, examples, procedural instruction or feedback?
- How do guidance type, timing, amount and participant control affect the experience?
- Under what conditions does guidance help people begin or continue, and when might it constrain expression, create pressure or interrupt engagement?
- How do effects differ by prior skill, confidence and session goal?

Prioritize direct evidence about adult sketching. Clearly label indirect evidence from other creative activities or educational settings. Distinguish optional guidance from mandatory instruction, and subjective experience from drawing quality.
