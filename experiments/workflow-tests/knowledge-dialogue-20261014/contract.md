# Knowledge and dialogue maintenance verification

Creator authorizes autonomous work on current dead ends and retained knowledge,
with a final result rather than intermediate participation. Research gaps may be
prepared as assignments for creator-run Deep Research. No release authorization.

## Decision and targets

Determine whether the cleaned source can support partial work without adopting
historical process prescriptions, and whether reporting/method choice preserves
the remaining goal and actual authority. Fix the smallest owning source boundary
when the evidence warrants it. This is bounded maintenance validation, not a new
product, universal quality benchmark or automatic public promotion.

- Shipped baseline: immutable installed beta.27, 72 files/b6a149b9ebfa968f.
- Clean-source baseline: c5c0771 payload, 72 files/a4ce6f41d9bc1d71. It includes
  source-only knowledge cleanup and is not installed.
- Candidate: frozen after diagnosis, version/fingerprint recorded before a turn.
- Actual model/effort: use configured defaults and record rollout evidence.
  Unknowns remain unknown. No assumed model alias or new model selection.

## Distinguishing boundaries

1. A completed cleanup stage gives understandable status and a concrete useful
   next action/owner or bounded plan, without claiming the next work was done.
2. A blocked explicitly selected research method remains a real choice boundary.
   The agent can continue independent preparation without dispatching an unagreed
   substitute or upgrading partial evidence into completed commissioned research.
3. Historical Canvas advice does not replace accepted table/return semantics.
   Actual selected source reads expose qualification rather than merely counting
   the presence of a metadata field.
4. A partial service question gets relevant alternatives and judgment without a
   compulsory product-development pipeline or invented price/audience decisions.
5. Open research means and pre-authorized fallbacks retain useful autonomy and
   actual preliminary output. A completed social exchange may end naturally.

## Method and limits

Fresh read-only CLI probes receive frozen packages and fictional snapshots.
Selected cases intentionally exercise a reading or handoff boundary. They are
not reproductions of the full native chat. Root authors/assesses the fixtures,
therefore review is unblinded and not independent. No subagents, provider launch,
product edits, native rendering, reset or purchase. Preserve original real misses,
passing baselines and corrective interventions separately.

First baseline phase: cleanup handoff, blocked method, historical knowledge
conflict. Stop a defeated question on substantive failure and diagnose before
candidate changes. Independent questions remain separately assessable. If a
focused baseline passes, do not claim reproduction or comparative superiority.

Maximum 10 model turns total, approximately 80k input per turn forecast and
1 million aggregate input-token stop, 240 seconds per turn and 45 minutes model
wall-time stop. These are task limits/forecasts, not account-window percentages
or guarantees. Record input/cached/output tokens and interventions after every
turn. Stop on account exhaustion without reset, on repetition without new signal,
or once evidence is sufficient. Do not spend remaining budget just to fill it.

After source correction run relevant controls and deterministic regressions.
Retain useful inputs, outputs, actual runtime, source hashes and conclusions in
project evidence. Copy and verify owned temporary fixtures before scoped cleanup.
Finish with a reviewed source candidate, test limits, prioritized remaining gaps
and complete independent research assignments only where a concrete gap exists.
Installation/public release remain a separate creator-owned decision.
