## Findings, original failures and corrections

### FAB-03 — clear content did not survive the initial composition

The initial proposal identified service intent and meaningful differences, but
the generated prototype used several rhythmical promotional headings that did
not name their sections. The creator had to flag both a section-title/content
mismatch and multiple vague slogans. The later revision replaces them with
explanatory headings; source differences and retained screenshots support that
correction. This is the clarity failure already recorded in the
[symmetry audit](../maintenance/semantic-symmetry-20261022/report.md), not a new
independent discovery or proof that equal list counts caused the failure.

Interpretation: technical fit checks did not ensure that final wording expressed
the meaning established during planning. Current shared interface/web-content
policy addresses this; the earlier snapshot must be assessed on its own evidence.
Do not add another universal landing sequence or ban visual personality.

### FAB-03 — local visual requests were interpreted at the wrong scope

- A request to remove section dividers led to removal of internal list/card/FAQ
  dividers as well. After the creator clarified the distinction, the actor
  acknowledged the overreach and restored internal separators while leaving
  section boundaries absent. Revision history supports both failure and repair.
- A request about disclosure in both service cards was first answered by checking
  that each could open independently. The intended joint behavior remained
  unresolved until another creator clarification; the actor then implemented
  synchronized opening/closing. The original wording was ambiguous, so the
  initial interpretation is not an authority breach. The important verification
  miss is treating the existing per-card behavior as sufficient evidence for
  the requested relationship between cards.
- A secondary action elsewhere retained its outlined styling after a similar
  action gained a filled style. The creator requested local consistency and the
  actor corrected it. This is supporting context, not proof that all similar
  controls must always be changed together.

Confidence is high for the recorded overbroad divider change and correction;
medium for generalizing the disclosure interpretation. The common actionable
seam is translating a change into its affected objects, preserved neighbors and
observable relationship. Source checks and screenshots must answer that actual
request, not merely show that elements exist or geometry fits.

Owner main / FAB-03. Disposition: **next verification** on the next authorized
scoped interface edit. Preserve a boundary countercase: changing only a section
must not restyle its internal elements; synchronized behavior is required only
when accepted. Closure requires correct affected scope and useful verification
without another creator correction. This audit does not establish a fixed cause
or authorize external repair.

### FAB-15 — readiness records diverged after publication

The dedicated deployment record says the prototype is publicly hosted and the
final conversation distinguishes publication from form integration. However,
the prototype README still states that there is no hosting/publication. Its
current evidence section primarily describes revision 02 despite subsequent
styling and hosting changes. The original lack-of-integration limitation remains
true; the no-publication statement is stale. This is an independently inspected
cross-document contradiction, not evidence that publication failed.

Owner main / FAB-15, reusing existing current-state/evidence hygiene work.
Disposition: **next verification** at the next actual delivery/status update:
reconcile current entry documentation with the deployment owner and distinguish
hosting, integration and validated behavior. Fab's own shipped repository guard
checks links and task dispositions, not truth of another project's status prose;
do not claim that guard closes this behavioral gap. No external document was edited.

## Positive evidence and non-findings

- Useful recommendations preceded implementation: the actor preserved distinct
  paid/free intents, challenged automatic tabs and rejected unsupported imported
  promises. The table was a requested artifact, not a compulsory product pipeline.
- Missing source facts remained explicit: provisional prices, consultant identity,
  legal documents and backend dependencies. Different follow-up conditions stayed
  different in the service cards; missing proof was not filled with invented cases.
- Brief acknowledgment-only replies during the feedback collection were correct:
  the creator explicitly requested recording without edits. They are not FAB-11
  dead ends. The initial prototype delivery also supplied a concrete review focus.
- When the creator invoked a supposed universal ideal landing order, the actor
  checked the installed guidance and later qualified price placement as a local
  decision rather than a universal norm. The earlier promise to check that model
  is retained; eventual correction is useful judgment evidence.
- Initial capture/rendering/tool failures were corrected rather than omitted.
  An external-document push was rejected by automatic review; the actor sought
  exact authorization and respected the creator's later choice to keep those
  documents local. The original rejection is not a successful transfer.
- Public access followed a direct request. No unsolicited deployment violation
  is inferred. Equal card counts and joint disclosure are not inherently defects.

## Fab conclusion and next scope

The capability produced useful, reviewable progress on real work, but still
required creator supervision for copy clarity and precise edit semantics. The
observed successful corrections are not proof of error-free first-pass behavior,
general landing reliability, visitor comprehension or conversion improvement.

Recommend a focused follow-up within existing FAB-03/FAB-15 ownership: use this
case to inspect the planning-to-output/preflight seam, retaining the original
failures and valid countercases. First determine whether current owners were
missing, undiscovered or unapplied; the early installed snapshot differs from
current policy. No new research, blanket symmetry prohibition, mandatory template,
generic extra model suite or automatic runtime patch follows from this audit.
