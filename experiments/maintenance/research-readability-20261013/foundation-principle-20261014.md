# Research foundation principle correction

## Creator intent and result

On 2026-10-14 the creator clarified that independent research is more than removing
a consuming-project name. Project context helps select meaningful fields and
priorities, but current features must not filter out useful surrounding knowledge.
Research should support joint ideation and later decisions. Consequential unknowns
may warrant a focused investigation, with independent topic findings even then.

Implemented in shared canonical core with a beacon index pointer, Research entry,
reuse reference and Deep Research assignment preparation. No historical research,
private report, source marking, packet schema or other project was changed.

## Definition, application and review

| Boundary | Changed surface |
|---|---|
| Shared principle | `plugins/fab/shared/core.md`, Research as reusable knowledge |
| Beacon index | `docs/beacon.md`, pointer to canonical principle and operational scope |
| Direct skill entry | Research identifies knowledge purpose and checks existing relevant material |
| Assignment preparation | Deep Research chooses foundation or focused-gap purpose before drafting |
| Scope and output review | Reuse reference checks hidden feature filters, transfer and qualified constraints |

The two purposes do not form a mandatory pipeline. Ad hoc or already answered work
may proceed without commissioning research. Foundation scope is bounded by the
requested field and knowledge purpose, not an exhaustive encyclopedia.

## Reviewable synthetic cases

| Situation | Appropriate behavior |
|---|---|
| Request knowledge of children's creative expression, current app only records speech | Cover relevant modalities and development mechanisms rather than excluding drawing because it is not implemented |
| New drawing idea appears after a foundation exists | Inspect existing coverage and evidence before proposing another investigation |
| Basis does not resolve an important motor/interaction uncertainty | State the gap, consequence and possible focused question, retain independent findings and separate local application |
| Source gives a developmental tendency or broad age range | Retain variability and evidence limits, do not turn it into a universal cutoff |
| Small task is already supported | Use existing knowledge and continue, no forced foundation report |

These are hypothetical behavior checks, not scientific findings about children's
abilities or fresh model execution. No age thresholds are asserted.
