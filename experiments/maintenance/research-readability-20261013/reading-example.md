## Before

A team must choose between guided and free-entry logging. In a fictional six-person pilot, four participants completed guided logging and two completed free entry. Guided logging used required fields and participants said these made it easier to start, but two described them as restrictive. Free entry allowed an unrestricted account, although its two completers both already kept journals, so completion cannot be attributed to the interface. The pilot observed one session per person and did not measure repeat use. A guided version should be considered for a second trial, not adopted as a proven retention solution. The next trial needs matched prior experience, equal task time and repeat sessions. The fictional participant quote was “Prompts helped me start; I still wanted room to explain.” The fixture identifier is `session;A`. Both paths should permit saving a partial entry. The team accepted partial saving, but has not selected an entry mode.

## After: main reader view

### Decision and evidence boundary

Compare guided and free-entry logging in a second trial. This is a recommendation
from a fictional pilot, not proof that guided logging improves retention.
Partial saving is accepted. The entry mode remains undecided.

attributed to the interface. The pilot observed one session per person and did
not measure repeat use.

The guided version merits another trial because it helped people start. Its
restrictive fields remain a concern that the next trial must examine.

- Give equal task time.

## Example assignment design

**Question:** Which entry mode merits a second trial, and what would the trial
need to distinguish ease of starting from repeat use?

**Evidence scope:** Compare completion, prior experience, required fields,
participant objections and the observation period. Keep unknowns explicit.
Do not infer retention from a single session. Preserve accepted partial saving.

**Presentation plan:** Start with the candidate and main uncertainty. Use a
compact comparison table for mode/completion/experience, short prose for the
confound and a list for trial conditions. Keep exact quotes and detailed session
records separately. Append the current Deep Research output contract in the
requested report language. This is an assignment example, not conducted research.
