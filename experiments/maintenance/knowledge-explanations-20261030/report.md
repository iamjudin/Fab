## Canonical mechanism changes

- Research ingestion distinguishes explanatory preservation from assigning source
  headings to owners. Mechanisms, relationships, alternatives and useful examples
  survive qualification. Fictional/unexecuted status constrains what an example
  proves, not whether its reasoning can be taught.
- Learning closure explicitly checks positive explanation alongside conditions,
  provenance and exceptions. A rule-contrast success is not connected explanation.
- Brainstorm/dialogue distinguishes a supported technical basis from a local
  choice, and requires support for the claims made rather than any related read.
  One-question pacing does not require manufacturing a foundational question.
- Workstream handoff treats an inherited next question as a proposal to assess.
  Recovery review checks whether continuation serves the goal as well as whether
  it repeats the packet. This directly addresses main's contribution to the miss.

The actual reader/compiler, schemas and source packets are unchanged. Associations
remain selective leads. No whole-graph preload, new mandatory checklist/form,
automatic archive fallback or repeated policy copy across ten skills is added.
