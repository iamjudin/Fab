## Cause and correction

Ingestion retained general reasoning, local workflow proposals, examples and dated
guidance in global-method packets. The existing source-authority label did not
explain those differences when the reader emitted a section. Some briefs also
called a former project's choices the maintained policy.

The correction is in the actual reading path, not a cosmetic project-name rename:

- Each brief presents knowledge and its qualifications separately from current
  application. The application links the owning active Fab contracts.
- Optional `readingContext` metadata labels section use. The reader displays a
  compact kind in the index and a qualified source boundary in selected reads.
  Source-synthesis is not a certification of independent or universal knowledge.
- Mixed content explicitly requires separating transferable reasoning from local
  prescriptions. Historical proposals remain available for comparison/provenance.
  They do not mandate the former full pipeline, Canvas, stage approvals or artifacts.
- The reader and package guard reject unknown or unexplained contexts. Existing
  unannotated packet output remains compatible. No new required user-report schema.
- Classification leaves every original section field, ID, exclusion list and
  source/retained content fingerprint identical to the pre-edit packet.

This addresses interpretation and active-source clutter. It does not reconstruct
the domain knowledge omitted by earlier project framing or verify every claim.
