# Fab0.1.0-beta.25 — readable and reusable research

- Research plans evidence and its reading structure together. Compact comparisons,
  independent field lists and short causal paragraphs replace dense repeated text.
- New Deep Research assignments use a localized output contract and topic-specific
  presentation plan. New reader prose avoids semicolons. Editable diagrams require
  actual width/readability checks, not just a top-to-bottom direction.
- Independent topic reports retain applicable conditions, with consuming-project
  names and decisions in a separate application. New source filenames stay topical.
  Optional authorized usage tags/provenance remain additive. Existing sources and
  labels are preserved, with no migration or new packet schema.
- Returned-output review distinguishes missing coverage/support, inconsistent units
  or arithmetic and presentation defects. Original results remain preserved.

## Limits

Two creator-run external outputs provide partial evidence. Independent framing
and experiment fields improved, but source, numerical and layout failures remain.
Fab does not guarantee external Deep Research compliance or decision-grade results.
Topic independence is not automatic personal-data anonymization. Named competitors
and evidence sources remain attributable. Cross-project use requires applicability
review. Bookworm task is prepared only. Canvas remains experimental.
