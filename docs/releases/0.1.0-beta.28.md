# Fab 0.1.0-beta.28 — clearer knowledge and useful handoffs

Research knowledge is easier to use without mistaking old project examples for
current rules. Completed stages identify the next action, its owner and authority;
already authorized work continues, while finished small conversations can end.

- Nine knowledge briefs distinguish reusable findings from current Fab application.
  All437original packet sections retain their text and IDs, with reading context
  for historical, mixed, dated and example material.
- The research reader protects the original file, UTF-8 line endings and exclusion
  intent, indexes fenced examples correctly, and validates hashes and authority.
  Package checks connect the registry, actual brief/full pairs and method index.
- Research preserves an explicitly chosen method when access fails, with separate
  autonomy for open means or an already approved fallback. Reader links and bundled
  mobile knowledge discovery are corrected.
- Stage reports turn pending work into a concrete action/owner or short plan.
  Source-usage checks cover refusal, additive second-project use and changed revision
  without silently changing other consumers or accepted decisions.

## Verification and limits

Sixty-six regressions, package and scoped release checks cover the current payload.
Ten knowledge/dialogue turns and eleven backlog-verification invocations use
GPT-6.1 Sol/Medium. Candidate cases pass within their recorded scope; several
baselines also pass. The short resumed dialogue does not reproduce native dead
ends, establish superiority or prove long-context stability.

A generated plugin inherits self-contained cleanup instructions and passes final
helper checks after a retained Python3.9 harness correction. Fresh generated-only
model cleanup remains unobserved. Knowledge integrity is not scientific source
verification; returned Deep Research source/numeric/layout compliance remains partial.

Canvas remains experimental with known overlap, default structure uses tables,
and Bookworm remains separate. No automatic existing-chat reload, installed-model
execution, native rendering or universal quality guarantee. Previous tags are immutable.
