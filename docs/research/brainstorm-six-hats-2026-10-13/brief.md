# Brainstorm strategy research — operational brief

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: proposal, no runtime change.

Follow-up: creator authorized an experimental dialogue candidate and tests after
clarifying that the goal is material new thinking, reasoned critique and useful
questions, not a framework mask. [Sixteen-turn comparison](../../../experiments/workflow-tests/brainstorm-dialogue-20261013/results.md)
shows useful baseline and v1 behavior but no established quality advantage.
V1 remains isolated/uninstalled; maintenance runtime is unchanged. The research
and indexed synthesis below preserve the pre-experiment reasoning and limits.

Further follow-up: [contextual corrective](../../../experiments/workflow-tests/brainstorm-context-20261013/results.md)
finds the narrower issue of bringing known evidence into an incremental answer.
V2 checks consequential changes of meaning/effort/agency and passes a same-context
corrective plus cosmetic proportion control. Six turns, one reconstructed episode;
promotion and ordinary-use stability remain open. The correction is not a full-hat
framework, and all initial targets had already read the supplied research.

Varied v2 validation: [five situations/seven turns](../../../experiments/workflow-tests/brainstorm-v2-situations-20261013/results.md)
now covers criticism, sound agreement, open exploration, feedback and accepted
constraints. Recommend a limited everyday trial after explicit promotion authority.
This tests v2 directly; observation of the older installed project is a separate
workstream, not a gate for virtual tests. A copy-anchor caveat remains visible.

Decision: how to add perspective and generation strategies without turning every
Brainstorm request into a mandatory workshop.

Recommendation: retain one Brainstorm skill; choose between opening possibilities,
examining an idea and comparing options. Add Six Hats as an optional tailored
perspective technique alongside question reframing. Use plain conversation by
default and visible hats on request or when the structure helps.

Grounds: current instructions state desired outcomes but give little strategy
selection guidance. The original method supports switching thinking directions
and situation-specific sequences. Reviewed human/LLM evidence is limited and
does not establish superiority over current Fab. PTFA particularly warns through
observed failures about generating more ideas after participants want to converge.

Creator feedback: all three pains apply — repetitive ideas, premature convergence
and weak dialogue/critique. Prefer a coherent conversation-mode candidate over
a single new report format.

Limits: precise causes have not yet been diagnosed from failing examples. Earlier bounded
Brainstorm outputs were useful under their contracts. No candidate behavior was
implemented or tested. Research findings are not accepted product decisions.
