## Source and reader review

Twelve primary source records distinguish theory, human experiments, synthetic
experiments, a meta-analysis and practitioner guidance. Direct Nature HTML and
one arXiv HTML variant failed, primary PDFs were reviewed instead. This changes
the access surface, not the agreed research method or the reported evidence type.
Study statistics are attributed to original papers, not re-estimated here.

The report openings and dense sections were reviewed for clear questions,
conditions, comparisons and uncertainty. Tables have three short-field columns,
no Mermaid, no semicolons or raw numeric citation markers in authored reader text.
Exact quotations/figures are not copied. Native Obsidian rendering and actual
human readability remain unobserved. The Russian local synthesis is a reader
view of the same work, not independent corroboration.
