## Root semantic review

Willingness cannot replace missing access or skill. A reward can communicate
support or pressure depending on context. A credential needs trustworthy criteria.
Competition can be appropriate or counterproductive, no universal ban follows.
A finite outcome can be better than endless return. An unexpected user strategy
can be useful rather than automatically cheating. An employee ranking is not
made voluntary by playful visuals. Early comprehension does not establish
long-term behavioral or learning benefit.

These are maintainer review contrasts, not participant observations or a new
empirical study. [Result](../../experiments/maintenance/knowledge-gamification-20261029/report.md).
