## Review and limits

Short wording can be clear in context. A visible label is not the only route to
accessible meaning. An error message must not invent a cause or password rule.
Punctuation normalization must preserve code/identifiers. Unbreakable groups and
hard breaks need adaptation checks. Browser spacing, native scaling and optical
alignment are different properties. No fixed grid, font, leading or line length
becomes a universal defect threshold.

These are root semantic contrasts, not a new rendering/participant study.
The original incomplete attribution, contradictory spacing recipes, numeric
uncertainty and missing requested Birman comparison remain explicit.
Source reconciliation does not claim that missing research has been conducted.
