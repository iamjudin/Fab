## Reconciled tensions and retained limits

- The historical catalogue counts, reuse thresholds, fixed rollout, mandatory
  three layers and enterprise tool roster are qualified, not active validity rules.
- Figma state variants can document appearance. Runtime transient state need not
  become public caller props; valid controlled APIs and finite variants remain valid.
- Domain-local composition and deliberate raw values can be legitimate. Shared
  intent is not forced pixel/API parity, a universal wrapper or a fake second target.
- Existing component/state/naming/ownership/recovery knowledge remains the owner.
  New definitions fill decision gaps; no second operational summary remains in briefs.
- The old DTCG-labelled example is not repaired wholesale by replacing dimensions.
  Selected versioned format/color evidence qualifies resolved values and transforms.
- General direction acceptance does not authorize new domain meaning or publication.
  Source-proposed approval/schema/journal ceremonies do not override task authority.
- The native/web authors lacked the companion foundation. Their illustrative
  roles, namespaces and independent APIs are reconciled by meaning, not concatenated
  into a supposedly compiled shared library.
- Original93/125/100unresolved export-marker mappings in agent/native/web reports,
  unpinned company cases, unexecuted snippets, platform/version/licensing uncertainty
  and missing empirical reliability/cost evidence remain explicit.
- Source reconciliation finishes knowledge disposition, not the report's proposed
  Figma mutations, compilation, user/assistive tests, scalability or productivity study.
