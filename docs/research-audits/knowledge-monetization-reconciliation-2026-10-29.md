## Maintainer semantic contrasts

A rarely used service can still provide continuing value. A frequently opened
local utility need not require subscription. SaaS is not a price mechanism.
Free can be a deliberate outcome and freemium need not be mandatory.
An honestly disclosed paid-only task differs from a surprise broken free promise.
Trial clocks can depend on evaluation opportunity or actual early costs.
Renewal cancellation, expiry and deletion differ. Unknown payment cannot become
a fresh charge merely because a dialog closed. A historical regional rule or
announced law cannot become a current universal compliant flag.
Improved local conversion can coexist with worse net value and operational cost.

These are root-authored review contrasts, not new causal, participant or provider
tests. Final cross-family application review remains open.
