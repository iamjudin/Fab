## Independent primary checks

Checked on 2026-10-16, separately from supplied evidence:

- [GitHub archive stability](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/working-with-files/using-files/downloading-source-code-archives)
  distinguishes moving refs, file contents at an unchanged commit and compressed
  archive bytes. The source's claim that tag archives cannot change is too broad.
- [Immutable releases](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/concepts/supply-chain-security/immutable-releases)
  protects assets and tag while the Release exists. Title/notes and prerelease/latest
  status remain editable. Deleting the Release is distinguished from changing its
  protected artifacts, and a formerly used protected tag name cannot be reused.
- [actions/create-release](https://github.com/actions/create-release) and
  [actions/upload-release-asset](https://github.com/actions/upload-release-asset)
  both show archived status and an unmaintained notice. Preserve the supplied
  examples as dated material, without adopting them as current defaults.
