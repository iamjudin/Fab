## Maintainer contrasts and remaining limits

One product can mix data-authority modes. Replica convergence is not business
conflict resolution. A local reminder need not imply a remote sender. Exact
delivery differs from eventual work. A fake widget, store Boolean and timer do
not establish the corresponding real capability. A bounded technical answer can
leave product behavior unchanged, while changed promises need affected updates.

No new model, device, store, server or external-standard check is performed.
Source23unresolved bindings, historical examples and platform-version limits
remain explicit. Source reconciliation is not fresh implementation proof.
