## Maintainer review contrasts

- Sample data can serve an evaluation job while not proving real-data output.
- Required identity, safety or data can justify a pre-result step. Familiar
  onboarding convention and future marketing segmentation alone cannot.
- Invitee and owner paths can differ. A reliably known role need not be asked.
- Early useful value and demonstrated mechanism do not establish a lasting effect.
- Explanation can be necessary before action, while optional tours can be deferred.
- An A/B variant effect is distinct from mediation by an activation event.
