# Beta limits

- Mobile apps are the current focus. Browser prototypes have evidence for selected
  interactions; native, device, offline, payment and production claims require
  their own checks.
- Structure and scenarios default to Markdown tables. Canvas previews are opt-in
  with known label overlap; native Obsidian rendering is unverified.
- Fresh-context tests support bounded tasks and partial routes, not every input
  or every changed instruction in the current version.
- Sustained continuity and the last queued Canvas-chat recovery remain open.
- Research citation coverage is partial. Recheck changing platform, legal and
  economic claims against current primary sources.
- Research refusal/additive/revision writes pass three bounded source cases, not
  universal cross-project efficacy. Generated cleanup rules are self-contained,
  but fresh generated-only model cleanup and transient preview disposal still
  require observation.

Detailed evidence and historical failures remain in the [backlog](backlog.md)
and [release policy](release-policy.md). Publication does not close those gaps.

- Research reader/framing and returned-output review contracts have bounded evidence.
  External Deep Research may still miss the contract or return unsupported,
  numerically inconsistent or dense output. Review before project use. Independent
  topic framing is not automatic personal-data redaction or applicability proof.
  Bookworm engineering task is prepared, with no implementation/behavior claim.

- Knowledge packet/registry integrity and source retention are structurally checked,
  not scientifically revalidated. Short source and shipped-baseline dialogue tests
  both pass; ordinary-use dead ends and sustained usefulness remain open.
